# Progression gist issues

Discrepancies between [The Shim Sham Plan](https://gist.github.com/keesey/7ae2c20287b0555a44d3f910eecb4530) and calculated sheet values. The sheet follows the rules.

## AC from level 10

Gist AC uses the full Dex modifier (+5 from level 10). [Advanced Tempweave](https://2e.aonsrd.com/equipment/armor/11-tempweave) has Dex Cap +4, so [Armor Class](https://2e.aonsrd.com/rules/343-armor-class) should cap Dex at +4.

| Level | Gist | Sheet |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | 29 | 28 |
| 11 | 30 | 29 |
| 12 | 31 | 30 |
| 13 | 34 | 33 |
| 14 | 35 | 34 |
| 15 | 36 | 35 |

## Will at level 15

Gist Will is +22. Calculated expert Will is +21 (Wis 1 + level 15 + 4 + Resilient 1). [Reinforced Ego](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=63) (Will master) is level 17, past the plan. Level 15 attribute boosts are CHA, CON, INT, STR — not Wis.

## Perception

[Perception](https://2e.aonsrd.com/rules/346-perception) is Wis 1 + proficiency (expert, master at 11). No other bonuses. The gist is 1 high at most levels, 2 high at 3–5, and matches at 6 (where it also drops from the level 5 gist value).

## Class DC

[Class DC](https://2e.aonsrd.com/rules/89-class-dc) is 10 + class DC proficiency + Dex (swashbuckler key attribute). Trained at 1; [Swashbuckler Expertise](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=63) makes it expert at 9. The gist is 1 high at 1–5, matches at 6–8, then stays trained and (from 10) also keeps Dex at +4.
